Questions

Before you book

Which areas do you cover?

The whole of North West England: Greater Manchester, Lancashire, Merseyside, Cheshire and Cumbria.

How do your quotes work?

Tell us about the job by phone, WhatsApp, email or the quote form. We pop round to look at the roof, or price small jobs from good photos, then give you a free, competitive quote before any work starts.

Do you only do roofing?

No. As well as roofs, guttering and chimneys, we take on building work such as extensions, porches and canopies.

Do you take on small repairs?

Yes. A slipped tile or a leaking gutter joint is as welcome as a full re-roof.

How do I contact you?

Call or WhatsApp 07348 426709, email joesrnb@gmail.com, or send the quote form.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe the problem and we will tell you. Call 07348 426709 or send the quote form.
