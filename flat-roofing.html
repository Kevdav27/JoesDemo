Flat roofing

New flat roofs and repairs for garages, extensions, dormers and porches across the North West.

Request a quote Call 07348 426709

Flat roofs fail at the edges and the falls

Most flat roof leaks start where the covering meets a wall, at the edge trims, or where water sits because the deck has sagged.

A proper replacement deals with the deck and the falls as well as the top layer, so water runs off and does not stand.

What the job covers

  • Old covering stripped and the deck checked
  • Rotten boards replaced and falls corrected so water runs off
  • A new waterproof covering, with the options explained on the visit
  • Upstands, edge trims and wall flashings
  • Outlets and guttering connected
  • Garages, extensions, dormers, porches and outbuildings

Signs a flat roof is failing

Water sitting days after rain

Standing water means the falls have gone. It finds the smallest gap and shortens the life of the covering.

Blisters, splits or lifting edges

Once the surface cracks or the edges lift, water gets under the covering and into the boards.

Damp on the ceiling below

By the time a stain shows indoors the deck is usually wet. It is worth a look before the boards rot.

Questions

Questions about flat roofing

Can a flat roof be repaired, or does it need replacing?

A single split or a failed joint can often be repaired. If the boards underneath are wet or the covering is brittle all over, replacement is the better use of money.

Which flat roof covering is best?

It depends on the size of the roof, what passes through it and how it is used. We explain the options and what each costs when we visit.

Can you do the garage and the extension together?

Yes. Tell us about both on the quote form and we will price them together.
