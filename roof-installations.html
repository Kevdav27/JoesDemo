New roofs and re-roofs

Full roof replacements in tile and slate for houses, bungalows and extensions across the North West, priced before work starts.

Request a quote Call 07348 426709

A whole roof, not another patch

When repairs keep coming back, the covering has usually reached the end of its life. A re-roof strips the old tiles or slates, checks the timbers underneath, and builds the roof back up in the right order.

On new builds and extensions we fit the roof from the bare rafters.

What the job covers

  • Old tiles or slates stripped and taken away
  • Rafters and timbers checked, with any rot shown to you before it is fixed
  • New breathable membrane and treated battens
  • Concrete tiles, clay tiles or slate, chosen to suit the house and the street
  • Ridges, hips, verges and valleys finished to suit the roof
  • Lead flashing around chimneys and where the roof meets a wall

Replacing a large part of a roof usually falls under Building Regulations. We explain what applies to your job when we quote.

Signs a roof needs replacing

Tiles slipping in several places

One slipped tile is a repair. The same problem across the roof usually means the fixings or battens underneath have failed.

Daylight or damp in the loft

Pinpricks of light, dark stains on the timbers or wet insulation all point to a covering that is letting water through.

A roof that dips or sags

A ridge that is no longer straight needs looking at before more weight or water makes it worse.

Questions

Questions about roof installations and re-roofs

How long does a re-roof take?

It depends on the size of the roof, the access and the weather. You get an expected timescale with the quote, and the plan for keeping the house dry between working days is explained before we start.

Can you match my existing tiles?

We bring options that suit the house and the neighbouring roofs. On a semi or a terrace, a close match to next door usually looks best.

Will I need scaffolding?

Most full re-roofs do. The quote says clearly whether scaffolding is included.
