Roof repairs

Leaks, slipped tiles and storm damage found and fixed across the North West, with the cause explained before the repair is priced.

Request a quote Call 07348 426709

Find the cause, then fix it

A damp patch on a ceiling is rarely directly under the fault. Water runs along felt and timbers before it shows. We trace it back to where it gets in, so the repair deals with the cause and not just the stain.

If a repair will not last because the whole covering is worn out, we say so and price both options.

What the job covers

  • Slipped, cracked and missing tiles or slates replaced
  • Leaks traced and sealed where the water gets in
  • Ridge and hip tiles re-bedded or re-fixed
  • Valleys cleared and repaired
  • Flashing and pointing made good
  • Storm damage made safe

Signs a roof needs a repair

A stain that grows after rain

A ceiling mark that spreads or darkens in wet weather means water is still getting in.

Tiles or mortar on the ground

Pieces in the garden or the gutter after wind are the roof telling you where it is loose.

Daylight in the loft

If you can see out, rain can get in. It is usually a quick fix when caught early.

Questions

Questions about roof repairs

My roof is leaking now. What should I do?

Catch the water, move anything valuable, and call or WhatsApp 07348 426709. A photo of the ceiling and of the roof from the ground helps us understand the job before we arrive.

Is it worth repairing an old roof?

Often, yes. A sound roof with a few failed tiles is a repair. If the same problems keep returning across the roof, a re-roof can cost less over time. We show you what we find and you decide.

Can you help with a storm damage insurance claim?

We can give you a written quote and photos of the damage to pass to your insurer.
