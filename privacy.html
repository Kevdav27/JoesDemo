Privacy

How your details are used

Joe's Roofing & Building, Rear Unit/Yard, Pemberton, Wigan WN5 8DH. Phone 07348 426709. This notice was written for this website on 15 November 2026.

Who we are

Joe's Roofing & Building is a local roofing and building business with a yard at Rear Unit/Yard, Pemberton, Wigan WN5 8DH. For privacy questions, call or WhatsApp 07348 426709 or email joesrnb@gmail.com.

What you can send us

If you use the contact or quote form, you may give your name, phone number, email address, postcode and a description of the job. If you call, WhatsApp or email, we receive whatever you choose to say or send, including photos.

What this website does not do

The forms do not send those details to a server we control, and this site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. After the checks on the page, your browser opens WhatsApp or your email app with a draft. You send that message yourself. WhatsApp is provided by Meta, and your email by your own provider, under their terms.

The page may keep a timestamp in your browser's session storage so a double-click does not open several drafts. That timestamp is not your message, and it is not sent to us.

The Google map

The home and Locations pages show a map from Google Maps. When the map loads, your browser connects to Google, which may set its own cookies and receives your IP address, under Google's privacy policy. If you would rather it did not, the town names and "Open in Google Maps" links still work, and you can block third-party cookies in your browser.

Social media links

The Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google buttons are plain links. Nothing is loaded from those sites until you choose to open one.

The chat assistant

The chat button opens an automated assistant. It answers from information on this website and runs in your browser. What you type is not sent to us or stored, and it is gone when you leave the page. To reach a person, use the phone, WhatsApp or email.

Why we use the details

Only to reply to your enquiry or quote, arrange a visit, and do the work you agree. We do not sell the details.

How long they stay

A WhatsApp chat or email stays until you or we delete it. If you would like an enquiry deleted, contact us and say so.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you think your details have been misused.
